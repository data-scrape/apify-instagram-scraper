# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Apify Instagram Scraper?

Apify Instagram Scraper is an Apify Actor used to collect publicly available Instagram data such as profiles, posts, comments, hashtags, and engagement information.

---

## Is CoreClaw an alternative to Apify?

Yes.

CoreClaw provides production-ready Instagram APIs together with Google Maps, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, Amazon, Reddit, TikTok, Zillow, Walmart, and Indeed.
